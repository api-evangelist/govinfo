The GovInfo team worked hard over the summer months developing new features and functionality. The September 2026 GovInfo release delivered 71 individually tracked changes. Key highlights include expanding the Annual Reports Derived Collection to feature GPO and Congressional Committee Print publications, updating the Copy Bookmarkable URL feature, enhancing the Bulk Data Repository, and adding support for Excel files in the Congressional Hearings collection.
