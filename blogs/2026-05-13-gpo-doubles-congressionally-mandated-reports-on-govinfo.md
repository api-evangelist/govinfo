GovInfo now has more than 1,000 Congressionally Mandated Reports from 90 Federal agencies. This total has doubled since GPO announced reaching 500 Congressionally Mandated Reports in October 2024 and has continued to grow since GPO first made Congressionally Mandated Reports available online in December 2023.
