This year, Armed Forces Day  will be celebrated on Saturday, May 16th. It is a day to recognize the bravery and resilience of those who safeguard our freedoms and uphold peace around the world. Communities across the United States come together on this day to express their gratitude and support for the armed forces through events, ceremonies, and parades.
