In this series, we will highlight a few of the many publications on GovInfo that showcase works of art through different mediums, including photographs, paintings, and drawings. This third part of the series, “Art in U.S. Federal Government Buildings,” presents publications that highlight works of art in some of our nation's historic buildings.
